# E-Scooter Rules for German Trains (RE / S-Bahn / ÖPNV)

**Status as of late 2026.** Rules are not uniform. Always verify for the specific line/operator.

## Key Requirements for Legal Use (eKFV)
- Max speed: 20 km/h
- Max continuous power: 500 W
- Max weight (without rider): 55 kg
- Max dimensions: 70 cm width, 140 cm height, 200 cm length
- Must have ABE (Allgemeine Betriebserlaubnis) or Einzelbetriebserlaubnis
- Insurance plate (Versicherungskennzeichen) required
- Lights, bell, brakes according to regulation
- Newer vehicles (from ~2026/2027) may require indicators (Blinker)

## Mitnahme on Trains (relevant for RE6, S8 in NRW)

| Train type | General rule | Notes |
|------------|--------------|-------|
| **ICE / IC / EC** | Folded = Handgepäck, free | Do not remove or charge battery. Stow safely. |
| **RE / RB / S-Bahn (NRW Tarif)** | Folded often = Handgepäck, free | NRW-Tarif treats folded e-scooters as hand luggage. Individual operators can still exclude via Hausrecht. |
| **U-Bahn / Tram / Bus** | Frequently restricted or banned | Many cities banned them due to battery fire risk (e.g. Köln, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt). |

**Practical advice for a typical NRW rail commute:**
- Prefer compact folding models that stay small when folded.
- Target weight capacity ≥110–120 kg for a 105 kg rider (plus bag).
- Confirm current Beförderungsbedingungen of VRR / DB Regio / Abellio or whoever operates RE6 and S8.
- Staff can refuse transport if train is full or scooter is considered unsafe.
- Never charge the battery on the train.

**Sources to check live:**
- Verkehrsverbund (VRR for the area)
- Specific operator Beförderungsbedingungen
- eBike-Crew ÖPNV-Radar or similar for quick overviews

**Recommendation workflow:**
1. Confirm the scooter is eKFV-compliant and has ABE.
2. Check folded dimensions and weight.
3. Look up current rules for RE6 and S8 specifically.
4. Prefer models known to be accepted on regional trains in NRW.
