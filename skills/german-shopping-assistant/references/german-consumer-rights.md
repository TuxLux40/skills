# German Consumer Protection Basics for Shopping

Useful facts when recommending products or shops in Germany.

## Return Rights (Widerrufsrecht)
- Online purchases: 14 days right of withdrawal (Widerruf) from receipt of goods.
- Many shops offer longer (30 days or more).
- Exceptions: personalized items, perishable goods, opened software/media in some cases.

## Warranty (Gewährleistung) vs. Guarantee (Garantie)
- **Gewährleistung**: Legal 2-year warranty from seller for defects present at delivery. Stronger in first year (burden of proof on seller).
- **Garantie**: Voluntary manufacturer guarantee. Can be longer but terms vary. Prefer shops that handle claims easily.

## Pricing & Costs
- Prices on German sites usually include 19% VAT (MwSt.) for most goods.
- Shipping to Germany should be included in total cost comparison.
- Check for "Versandkostenfrei ab X €" thresholds.

## Seller Reliability
- Prefer known shops or "Versand durch Amazon" / marketplace sellers with high ratings.
- Check for German imprint (Impressum) and contact options.
- For expensive items: local pickup or established retailers reduce risk.

## Local Considerations (NRW)
- MediaMarkt, Saturn, expert, etc. have stores in the region for pickup and service.
- Public transport coverage is good; factor in delivery vs. local availability.
