# German Shopping Platforms & Price Comparison

Primary sources for research and price comparison in Germany.

| Platform | Best for | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **idealo.de** | Broad product comparison | #1 in Germany. Price history, many shops, shipping filters. Start here for most products. |
| **geizhals.de** | Electronics & hardware | Excellent filters for tech (GPUs, laptops, phones, e-scooters). Detailed specs. |
| **amazon.de** | Fast delivery, reviews | Check "Versand durch Amazon" for reliability. Compare with Idealo total price. |
| **MediaMarkt / Saturn** | Electronics, in-store pickup | Often weekend promotions ("MwSt. geschenkt"). Check local availability in NRW. |
| **Alternate, Mindfactory, Caseking** | PC hardware, gaming | Competitive prices, good for components. |
| **Otto, Kaufland, Lidl Online** | General, household | Otto strong on appliances; check return policies. |
| **mydealz.de** | Deals & coupons | Community-driven bargains. |
| **Check24** | Insurance, energy, some products | Useful for contracts alongside products. |

**Tips:**
- Always compare **total price** including shipping to Germany/NRW and any import costs.
- Prefer shops with German return rights (14 days Widerruf, longer for many online shops).
- For high-value items: check warranty (Garantie) and service network in Germany.
- Local availability: use shop locators or places_search for MediaMarkt/Saturn in NRW.
- Price history on Idealo is valuable for timing purchases.

**Search strategy:**
1. Idealo or Geizhals for overview and filters.
2. Cross-check top 3-5 offers on Amazon.de and official sites.
3. Verify current stock, shipping time, and seller ratings.
